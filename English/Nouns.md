# Nouns

Nouns/commoun nouns = name/place/etc. Basketball Player, country
Proper-nouns = (specific noun), Michael Jordan, France (first letter of name/title must be capitalized)

Do not use article (a, an, the) on proper-noun, unless necessary

## Usage

It can be used as:

- subject
- object
- possesive (ownership) `Maria's book`

### Combination

in some cases, nouns are used along with proper-noun to explain it
The famous scientist, Sir Isaac Newton

## Pronouns

Merupakan kata ganti sebuah noun. (She, he, it, they, I)

They bisa digunakan sebagai kata ganti kalo kita tidak tahu gender nya
Someone left their phone, `they` should come back for it.
The doctor will see you now. They are waiting in the office
